Mars Climate Orbiter (1999)

NASA’s investigation describes impulse data supplied in pound-force seconds (lbf·s) where the interface specification required newton-seconds (N·s). The specification existed; inconsistent data and failure to detect the mismatch contributed to the loss. The lesson is to respect and independently validate an interface, not merely write documentation. In our World Bank pipeline, the corresponding promises include persons, years and percentages.

Sources checked for Pass4: NASA mission, JPL findings, NASA investigation, AppendixB §§4–5 (PDF pages73–74).

Knight Capital (2012)

The SEC describes an incorrect deployment activating defective code: over four million erroneous orders in45minutes and a loss above$460million. Deployment, operational response and risk controls matter as well as local calculations. Unit tests alone are not claimed to have prevented this incident. Our local handover checks the saved candidate and complete report workflow, with an explicit limit on what those checks establish.

Primary source: SEC release2013-222, published16October2013 about1August2012.
